      How to use SkyShift

      What you are looking at

      Real infrared images from NASA’s SPHEREx telescope, which photographs the whole sky every six months. Each visit is a set of images of the same patch of sky taken at about the same time.

      Spot something that moved

      • Blink flips between two visits, A and B. Anything that jumps has moved.
      • What changed shows the difference: orange is brighter in B, blue is brighter in A. A moving object leaves a blue and orange pair.
      • Find movers looks for those pairs for you. The button shows what it found.
      • Click or tap an object, then Track & identify to follow it through every visit and check it against star catalogues.

      Find your way around

      • Search any star, galaxy or nebula by name, or enter RA and Dec.
      • Drag to move; scroll or pinch to zoom; click or tap a spot for details.
      • Layers changes the infrared band, switches on colour and adds catalogue markers. The menu at the top left has more tools.
